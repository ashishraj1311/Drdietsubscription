Dr Diet — plan photos
=====================

Drop a JPG for each plan here, named EXACTLY by its plan id + ".jpg".
The app loads it automatically; until a file exists it shows a branded
emoji tile instead. Recommended: ~1000x600, < 250 KB.

Filenames expected:
  p-lean.jpg         Lean Machine   (Non-Veg · lose weight)
  p-muscle.jpg       Muscle Fuel    (Non-Veg · gain muscle)
  p-balanced-veg.jpg Balanced Veg   (Veg · maintain)
  p-plant.jpg        Plant Powered  (Vegan · eat healthier)
  p-egg-fit.jpg      Egg-Fit Balance (Eggetarian · gain muscle)
